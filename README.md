One joke about Monday mornings, at most 200 characters — the whole entry.
`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) scores six fresh one-liners against the
judge's five criteria in the brief's own order (really about Monday
mornings, real laugh with a turn, surprise, economy of words, freshness)
and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`.

The winner:

> Monday called in sick. HR said no — apparently even Mondays can't escape Mondays.

81 characters, one line. The turn lands at the end: Monday itself tries to
skip Monday and gets refused — the day that ruins everyone else's morning
can't escape its own. No coffee, no alarm, no "I hate Mondays": the joke
is about Monday as a character, not a complaint about it.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

Two runs produce byte-identical output.
